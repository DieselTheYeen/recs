Creed — Declaration of Belief

We do not come seeking comfort, forgiveness or shelter from what we are. We come to be tested, to be broken, to be remade. The fire does not judge us for our weakness, it reveals. The trial does not exist to spare us, it exists to forge us. The scar is not proof of suffering, but proof of survival. We reject stagnation, self-pity, the lie that failure is final. Strength is earned. Worth is forged. Growth is demanded. We stand among those who have fallen and risen, those who have suffered and endured, those who refuse to remain what they once were. The world owes us nothing. So, we will become something it cannot ignore. Through ruin, we rise. Through hardship, we endure. Through fire, we become.

This is our Covenant.

Rank Progression System

You must complete five of the listed tasks to qualify.

Ravager → Vanguard

[size=x-small]➤ Win 5 sanctioned spars. ➤ Participate in 2 raids or pack battles. ➤ Train 2 Initiates in combat. ➤ Host 1 combat lesson or sparring workshop. ➤ Defeat an opponent ranked equal or higher than themselves. ➤ Represent the Covenant in an inter-pack duel or tournament. ➤ Complete one endurance challenge issued by leadership. ➤ Be recommended by the Sovereign or Warden for exceptional battlefield leadership.[/size] You must challenge the current Vanguard, this is a necessity.

Veil → Overseer

[size=x-small]➤ Conduct 5 scouting patrols. ➤ Discover 3 new territories or points of interest. ➤ Gather intelligence from 3 foreign packs. ➤ Escort visitors or diplomats on 2 occasions. ➤ Resolve or assist in one diplomatic encounter. ➤ Shadow another wolf without being detected (informant purposes). ➤ Submit regular intelligence reports to leadership. ➤ Train another Veil in reconnaissance or stealth.[/size]

Reaper → Pursuer

[size=x-small]➤ Lead 5 successful hunts. ➤ Feed the Covenant through large prey on 3 occasions. ➤ Teach one hunting lesson. ➤ Train 2 Initiates in tracking or hunting. ➤ Successfully track prey over multiple threads. ➤ Organize a seasonal hunting expedition. ➤ Assist during food shortages or pack events. ➤ Demonstrate exceptional knowledge of prey migration or survival.[/size]

Hallow → Physician

[size=x-small]➤ Treat 5 injured wolves. ➤ Successfully identify and use 10 medicinal herbs. ➤ Teach one healing lesson. ➤ Mentor 2 Penitent's interested in healing. ➤ Create or improve three medicinal remedies. ➤ Assist during a raid or large-scale battle. ➤ Expand the Covenant's herbal knowledge by discovering a new remedy or gathering location. ➤ Become recognized by leadership for exceptional medical knowledge.[/size]
